1 O God, come to my assistance; * O Lord, make haste to help me.
2 Let them be confounded and ashamed * that seek my soul:
3 Let them be turned backward, and blush for shame * that desire evils to me:
4 Let them be presently turned away blushing for shame * that say to me: Tis well, tis well.
5 Let all that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee; * and let such as love thy salvation say always: The Lord be magnified.
6 But I am needy and poor; * O God, help me.
7 Thou art my helper and my deliverer: * O Lord, make no delay.
