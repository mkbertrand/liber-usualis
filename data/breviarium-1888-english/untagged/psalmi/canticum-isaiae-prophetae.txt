1 O Lord, I will praise Thee, though Thou wast angry with me, * Thine anger is turned away, and Thou comfortest me.
2 Behold, God is my salvation: * I will trust and not be afraid:
3 For the Lord is my strength and my song: * He also is become my salvation.
4 Therefore with joy shall ye draw water out of the wells of the Saviour: * and in that day shall ye say: Praise the Lord and call upon His Name!
5 Declare His doings among the people, * tell them that His Name is exalted.
6 Sing unto the Lord, for He hath done glorious things: * make ye this known in all the earth.
7 Cry aloud and shout, thou inhabitant of Zion: * for great is the Holy One of Israël in the midst of thee!
