So far, I've likely logged over 3,000 hours to bring the Liber Usualis Project to where it is now. While the Latin text is complete for the entire liturgical year, it is likely that hundreds or thousands more hours of labor will be needed to complete other objectives. Major objectives include the English translation and other versions of the Breviary.

I, along with all other contributors, are purely volunteers. Since this is a project we care deeply about, all content is published free and open source, and no payment is required to use any features of the website. Monetary donations are very helpful both for defraying operational expenses and for ensuring that I am able to justify continuing to spend as much time as I do developing the website. Please consider a monthly, annual, or one-time donation to make this great and worthwhile work possible!
